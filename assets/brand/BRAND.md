# CodeGym Mark

CodeGym is a personal training space for developers who want to understand their code. Its personality is disciplined, approachable, and quietly technical. The mark primarily appears in a web header, training hub, and browser favicon.

The installed direction is the **bracket barbell**: square code brackets form weight plates, a bar connects them, and a slash suggests code. Two companion SVGs provide a shield lift for Training Grounds and cyber/RE paths, and a flexing byte for Learn and round-completion screens. The bracket barbell remains the primary identity and marks Practice. All three are used as a coordinated symbol family. These are original marks; KC7 supplies structural inspiration for the product, rather than logo artwork.

## Files

- `codegym-mark.svg`: transparent vector master, forest ink for light backgrounds.
- `codegym-mark-dark.svg`: lime mark for dark backgrounds.
- `codegym-mark-mono.svg`: black monochrome mark; `codegym-mark-white.svg`: white reversal.
- `codegym-wordmark.svg` and `codegym-wordmark-dark.svg`: symbol plus editable text.
- `codegym-shield.svg`, `codegym-flex.svg`: alternative concepts.
- `codegym-mark-light-{64,128,256,512}.png`, `codegym-mark-dark-{64,128,256,512}.png`: transparent raster exports.
- `favicon.svg`, `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon.ico`: compact favicon exports.
- `../../brand.html`: comparison board and small-size checks.

## Palette

| Color | Hex | RGB | HSL | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forest ink | #182A24 | 24, 42, 36 | 160°, 27%, 13% | Symbol, strong text, dark panels |
| Electric lime | #D9FC75 | 217, 252, 117 | 76°, 96%, 72% | Calls to action, reversed symbol |
| Chalk paper | #F4F1E9 | 244, 241, 233 | 44°, 33%, 94% | Page background |

The web wordmark uses Courier New: bold CODE, regular GYM. Editorial headings use Georgia; interface text uses Trebuchet MS. SVG text stays editable, so exact rendering depends on font availability. Convert text to outlines before a typography-critical print handoff.

## Placement

Use the symbol at 24 px or larger for normal UI; use the supplied favicon at 16–32 px. Keep the combination mark at least 160 px wide. Give the symbol clear space equal to roughly one quarter of its height. Use forest ink on light backgrounds and lime on forest ink. Monochrome black is suitable for single-color reproduction; a white reversal can be derived from the same geometry when needed.

Preserve the aspect ratio and original geometry. Avoid stretching, adding gradients or shadows, recoloring each stroke independently, or placing the transparent mark over busy content. Use the bracket barbell for compact identity; reserve the more detailed mascot for larger illustrations.

## Symbol Roles

| Symbol | Placement | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Bracket Barbell | Header, favicon, Practice tab and practice heading | Coding identity and deliberate repetition |
| Flexing Byte | Learn tab, library and lesson headers, round results | Guidance, learning, and encouragement |
| Shield Lift | Training Grounds tab and header, cyber and RE path cards | Applied judgment and investigation foundations |

Decorative instances have empty alternative text when adjacent text already names the section. Avoid adding symbols to every card or paragraph.
